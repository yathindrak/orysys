# Remote Access Policy

Employees may access internal systems remotely only from a bank-managed device with active endpoint protection. Multi-factor authentication is required for every remote session.

## Sensitive operations

Payment-production administration requires the administrator role and an approved change record. A normal viewer or analyst session must not perform account changes, credential rotation, or production deployment.

## Support

Lost devices must be reported to the service desk immediately. The security team can revoke the device session and associated credentials.
