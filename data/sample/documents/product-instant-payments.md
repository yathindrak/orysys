# Instant Payments Product Specification

The instant-payments product supports domestic transfers up to the configured customer limit. Every accepted request receives a stable transaction reference and an idempotent status endpoint.

## Availability target

The pilot target is 99.9 percent monthly availability. Planned maintenance and external clearing-network outages are reported separately.

## Customer communication

The assistant may explain documented status and policy. It must not promise reimbursement, alter a transfer, or provide personalized financial advice.
